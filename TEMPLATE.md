_Blameless postmortem: we look at how systems and processes allowed this to happen, not at who did what. Write about decisions and the information people had at the time, not about individuals._

## Summary

Two to four sentences: what happened, what the impact was, how it was mitigated.

## Impact

Who or what was affected, for how long, and how badly. Use measured numbers only; say "unknown" rather than guess.

## Detection

How we found out (alert, user report, chance). How could we have found out sooner?

## Root cause

The conditions that made this possible, not only the trigger. Ask "why" until you reach something the team can change. There is usually more than one contributing factor.
